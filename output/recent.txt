I'm satisfied that I closed the Nasdaq short when it almost reached my target because I was at the end of my rope and I had to leave the screen anyway.

I'm satisfied that I decided after the physiotherapy with Ron that I would not trade because I recognized that I have consumed my mental resources. I am in particular happy that even later on when I looked at the chart around 8-9 p.m., just before the stock market closed, I didn't engage in any trades. Even though the idea of shorting the Nasdaq again crossed my mind. And I am delighted that I have accepted the idea of perhaps missing out and I disconnected from trading anyway.

I learned today that the chart of the USD/JPY is particular and that the five-wave Elliott impulse I conceptualized didn't materialize in the way I thought it would.

A recurring pattern that I recognized was that immediately after the price of the USD Yen hit my stop loss, which was actually in profit, I initiated immediately two other short positions. My problem with that is that by the stop loss being hit in the existing positions, my initial Elliott Wave price target was invalidated and I didn't have new information of a new idea which would justify the new positions. I basically acted impulsively and based on the conviction that the price target would still be reached but maybe by a different mechanism/route.

Decision fatigue is real in trading with my mental and health challenges. Making lots of probability-based decisions, navigating uncertainty, and weighing pros and cons for any particular decision is draining. After a certain amount of such decisions my performance will deteriorate because my normal capacity has been exhausted.

I feel some frustration, disappointment, and regret regarding the Hun position for which I didn't set a stop-loss and it sunk something like 20% after earnings. I am thinking that for this sort of cyclical commodity-based stock, where I'm just accumulating for the sake of diversifying my portfolio / copying MC, maybe I should set a 10% stop-loss because I entered it not at a terrible level. If it drops 10% below that, that's no good.
